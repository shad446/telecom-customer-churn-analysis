## 📡 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

**Project Overview**

Developed an interactive analytics dashboard to investigate customer churn patterns and identify opportunities to improve customer retention in the telecommunications industry.

**Business Objectives**
- Analyze customer churn and retention patterns.
- Explore relationships between customer usage, billing, and churn.
- Identify customer segments that may require targeted retention strategies.
- Present actionable insights to support data-driven business decisions.

**Tools & Technologies**
- Python
- Pandas
- Plotly
- Streamlit

**Project Deliverables**
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard
- Customer churn analysis
- Data visualizations and business insights
